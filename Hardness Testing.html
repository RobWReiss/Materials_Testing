Rockwell Hardness Test Simulator

Press a tiny indenter into steel and measure how hard it pushes back.

New to this? What the words mean
Hardness
How well a steel resists being dented. Harder steel is usually stronger, but also more brittle.
The Rockwell test
An indenter is pressed into the surface. The machine measures how deep the dent is. A shallow dent means hard steel and a bigger number.
Preload and main load
A light load (10 kgf) seats the indenter and sets the zero. The heavy main load is then applied and removed, back to the light load. The depth of the dent left behind gives the reading.
Why test several times?
Steel is never perfectly uniform, so three indents are made, well apart from each other, and the average is used.

Why two scales? A diamond cone on soft steel digs so deep that the scale runs out. A steel ball on hard steel flattens and the dent is too shallow to measure properly. The two scales are different rulers, so an HRC number cannot be compared with an HRB number. If the display says OFF SCALE, you chose the wrong one.
