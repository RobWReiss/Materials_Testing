Charpy Impact Test Simulator

Swing the hammer, break the bar, and find out how tough the steel really is.

New to this? What the words mean
The Charpy test
A heavy pendulum swings down and breaks a small notched bar. The energy the bar soaks up, in joules (J), is its toughness. Tough steel absorbs a lot of energy; brittle steel snaps with very little.
Strength is not toughness
A very strong steel can still be brittle. Toughness tells you whether a part will bend and warn you, or shatter without warning.
Why temperature matters
Most steels get more brittle as they get colder. Steel that is tough in a warm workshop can crack like glass on a freezing night, so tests are done at the temperature the steel will really work at. Austenitic stainless steel (304) is the exception.
Fracture appearance
Tough breaks leave a dull, rough, fibrous face. Brittle breaks leave a bright, shiny, crystalline face. "% shear" is the share of the face that is dull and fibrous, not the shiny part.
KV300
The energy absorbed using a V-notch bar on a 300 J pendulum, as written in ISO 148-1.
The pass rule
Three bars make one test. The average must meet the requirement. One bar may fall below it, but none below 70% of it.

2. Judge the test

How to estimate % shear

  1. Ignore the dark strip at the top of each picture. That is the notch, cut before the test. The fracture face is the area below it.
  2. Find the shiny part: bright, sparkly, crystalline metal, usually a patch in the middle. This is the brittle part.
  3. The dull grey part around it, rough like torn metal, is the fibrous (tough) part. % shear is the share that is dull, not the share that is shiny.
  4. Tick "Show grid" to split the face into 20 squares of 5% each. Count the shiny area: a filled square counts 1, a half-filled square counts ½, and two halves make one. Ignore tiny slivers.
  5. Multiply your count by 5 to get % shiny, then subtract from 100 to get % shear. Example: 6 full and 4 half squares is 8 squares, so 40% shiny and 60% shear.
  6. All dull means 100% shear. All shiny means 0%. Being within 10 points is good enough.
